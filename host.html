        Optional: send texts automatically

        1. This part costs money. Skip it to keep the app free.
        2. Open a Twilio account, get a toll-free number and submit its verification. Twilio blocks texts to US phones until that is approved.
        3. In the Apps Script editor, open Project Settings, then Script properties. Add TWILIO_SID, TWILIO_TOKEN and TWILIO_FROM (your number, like +18335551234).
        4. Reload this page and send yourself a test from the Messages tab.
